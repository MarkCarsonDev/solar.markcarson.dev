Education

I graduated with my undergraduate degree, a B.Sc. Computer Science, with a GPA of 3.5 from California State University, Long Beach in December of 2024. But that's just my formal education; my time spent learning extends far before university and will continue to the day I die.

I first taught myself how to program using the resources available to me at the age of 14. My first project was a NodeJS bot to help me host fair and competitive pick-up games for my favorite video game at the time. From this, I built out an organization adn auxilary website to provide new users with the tools to improve.

It was with this large project that I learned the power of self-driven education. I continue to carry this principle to every problem I solve, and make considerable effort to remain adaptable to available tools and technologies.
